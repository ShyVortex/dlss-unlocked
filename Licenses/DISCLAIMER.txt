1. PROJECT NATURE & INTEROPERABILITY
------------------------------------
DLSS Unlocked is an independent, non-commercial, open-source interoperability tool
and compatibility wrapper. Its sole purpose is to enhance hardware feature
accessibility, performance, and compatibility by bridging modern graphics APIs
(DirectX 12, Vulkan) and vendor-agnostic upscaling / frame generation technologies
across supported hardware.

This project is developed and distributed pursuant to interoperability and
